UNDER THE HOOD

The math behind the move.

Monthly payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is principal, r is annual percentage rate ÷ 1,200, and n is years × 12. At a 0% rate, payment = P ÷ n.

Every month, interest = remaining balance × r. The rest of your payment, plus your extra, reduces the balance. The final payment is capped at the amount owed.

Both plans start with the same balance, rate, and term. Interest saved is the difference between their total interest; time saved is the difference in payment counts. Calculation uses full precision; displayed values are rounded.

All amounts are in USD. The starting numbers are an illustrative example. Local storage keeps your last inputs and up to six saved scenarios on this browser. Clear browser site data to erase them. No data is sent to a server.
