CALCULATION

Mortgage calculation

Monthly payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is principal, r is annual percentage rate ÷ 1,200, and n is years × 12. At a 0% rate, payment = P ÷ n.

Every month, interest = remaining balance × r. The rest of your payment, plus your extra, reduces the balance. The final payment is capped at the amount owed.

Both plans start with the same balance, rate, and term. Interest saved is the difference between their total interest; time saved is the difference in payment counts. Calculations use full precision. Displayed amounts are rounded.
